# vehicle

Meshopt copies of `assets-src/models/vehicle` (built by `scripts/compress-assets.mjs`). `car_*.glb` are the Blender library cars (see `assets-src/blender/README.md`); `sports_car.glb` and `police.glb` are the earlier generated bodies, no longer loaded since Phase 13d (kept until the owner agrees to delete them); `boat.glb` is the boat.
